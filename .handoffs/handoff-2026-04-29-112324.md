## Architecture and design notes

### The QA-as-I-go workflow (new this session)

Per user direction, every new room map gets a self-QA pass before sign-off:

1. **Author** the `room-map.md` + `room-map-photos.md` from blueprint + photos + annotated jpeg.
2. **QA against the blueprint first:**
   - Re-render the blueprint area at 400 DPI; look at it again with fresh eyes.
   - Check: walls named match what's drawn? Doors at right positions and right types (arc shape!)? Window positions? Fixture footprints?
   - Check: corner letters from the annotated jpeg in the right alphabetical positions?
   - Check: room dimensions match the dimension chains?
3. **THEN QA against the photos:**
   - Open at least one photo per corner (start frame minimum); add mid-sweep + end-sweep where ambiguous.
   - Check: does each wall feature in the map actually appear in the photos that should show it?
   - Check: does the photo→wall index point to the right photos?
   - Check: are TBD items resolvable from frames not yet looked at?
4. **Fix anything QA caught** (in-place edits, not full rewrites).
5. **Sign off** → list files touched, STOP per "always review firsts" rule, wait for user to acknowledge before next room.

This shape has now caught: kitchen corner-name bug (prior session, fixed at start of this session), washroom door-type misread, washroom dimensions, laundry door-type, missing PS pipe shaft. The QA gate is paying dividends — better to catch errors per-room than have them compound across 12 rooms.

### The corner-naming convention

For every room in this project:

- Walls are named by feature, NOT by direction (no "north wall"; instead `kitchen-wall`, `bath-wall`, `entry-wall`).
- Corner names are ALPHABETICAL pairs of the two adjacent walls: `corner-{wall-a}-{wall-b}` where wall-a < wall-b alphabetically. Example: `corner-bath-end`, NOT `corner-end-bath`.
- For non-rectangular rooms (currently corridor-1), the "4 corners" are an APPROXIMATION of the bounding-box. The map's overview should explicitly note the L-shape or other non-rectangular geometry.
- For rooms with non-4 photo capture (toilet-1-f with 2; living-dining with 6), the map still describes 4 walls + 4 corners (template consistency for the 3D code), but the photo→camera mapping section flexes to the actual capture.

1. `## overview` — what the room is, dimensions, materials
