## Problems encountered and how they were solved

### Problem 1: Window light + sun + ceiling point + envMap stacked = overexposed scene
- **Symptom:** "why is the lighting so bright?"
- **Root cause:** Outdoor sun at 2.2 + ceiling fill point at 12 + RectArea task + every material at envMapIntensity 1.0-1.6, all multiplied through HDRI envMap, all driven through ACESFilmic at exposure 1.05. Multiple unrelated additive light sources stacked.
- **Fix:** First pass dropped exposure to 0.7. After "make it look like a closed kitchen" command, full lighting rebuild — see Phase 5.
- **Lesson:** When using an HDRI as scene.environment for indoor scenes, the room must be enclosed (walls block sky illumination from above) AND `scene.environmentIntensity` must be dropped to 0.4-0.6 because materials still see the env globally regardless of geometry.

### Problem 2: "LED strip" appearance on cabinet caps
- **Symptom:** "why do the cupboards have LED strips?"
- **Root cause:** Brushed stainless caps were too polished (metalness 0.95, roughness 0.30, anisotropy 0.85). Plus envMapIntensity 1.4 amplified HDRI reflections. Plus bloom threshold 0.85 was low enough that focused specular peaks bloomed. The combination read as glowing strips.
- **Fix:** Dulled `matStainlessBrushed` and `matPlinth` (roughness up, metalness down, anisotropy down, envMapIntensity down). Raised bloom threshold to 1.05 — above any non-HDR specular peak.
- **Lesson:** Anisotropic specular + low bloom threshold + bright reflective material = glowing-strip artifact. Either keep one (anisotropy) and dial back the others, or vice versa.

### Problem 3: Sink basin opening hidden by solid counter
- **Symptom:** Couldn't see into the basin from above; counter covered it.
- **Root cause:** Counter was built as a single solid `BoxGeometry` slab over the entire kitchen run. No cutout for the sink.
- **Fix:** Replaced `makeCounterRun()` with `ExtrudeGeometry` on a rounded-rectangle Shape that supports an optional `cutouts` array. Each cutout is a Path hole punched into the counter's outline. Call site passes `{ cutouts: [{ x: sinkCx, z: 0.30, w: 0.78, d: 0.46, r: 0.030 }] }`.
- **Lesson:** For real countertop with sink/cooktop cutouts, use ExtrudeGeometry with hole Paths, not solid Box slabs.

### Problem 4: Sink basin had no hole in the floor (drain was a flat black disc)
- **Symptom:** "the sink doesnt have a hole"
- **Root cause:** Drain was a thin black `CylinderGeometry` placed on top of the solid bottom plate. Looked like a flat dark spot, not a hole.
- **Fix:** Modified the bottom plate `Shape` to include a circular `Path` hole at the drain location. ExtrudeGeometry now creates inner walls around the hole. Added a 50 mm-deep dark drain pipe stub below the hole + sealed bottom plate so looking down through the strainer reveals real depth.
- **Lesson:** When user says "no hole", they mean a real geometric void, not a darker color. Use `Shape.holes.push(Path)` for ExtrudeGeometry holes.

### Problem 5: Faucet facing the wrong way
- **Symptom:** "The faucet is also facing the wrong way"
- **Root cause:** Curve control points used `zBack - 0.20` for the spout end. With `zBack = 0.055` (at back of sink), the spout extended to z=-0.145 — INTO the back wall, away from the basin. The mental model was wrong: I had +Z as "back" but +Z is actually toward the camera (and the back wall is at z=0, sink at z=[0.07, 0.53]).
- **Fix:** Reversed all curve control point z-offsets. Spout now extends from `zBack = 0.055` forward to `zBack + REACH = 0.325` (near sink center).
- **Lesson:** When orienting things, always confirm coordinate convention from existing geometry. The kitchen has back wall at z=0 and the camera/user at high z, so "forward into the room" = +Z.

### Problem 6: Faucet handle disconnected from body
- **Symptom:** "the faucet's handle is not connected to the faucet" (with Mac-filesystem screenshot path I couldn't read)
- **Root cause:** Collar was at lever-local `(0.014, 0, 0)` with length 0.018 → collar inner edge at world `x = xCenter + 0.018 + 0.005 = xCenter + 0.023`. Tube radius 0.0165 → tube surface at `xCenter + 0.0165`. Gap of 6.5 mm.

### About three.js r184 + this codebase
- `MeshPhysicalMaterial` features that work in r184: `clearcoat`, `clearcoatRoughness`, `transmission`, `ior`, `thickness`, `attenuationColor`, `attenuationDistance`, `iridescence`, `iridescenceIOR`, `iridescenceThicknessRange`, `anisotropy`, `anisotropyRotation`. All confirmed working.
- `scene.environmentIntensity` is a single global multiplier on env contribution — much faster than walking all materials to adjust envMapIntensity individually.
- `RectAreaLight` requires `RectAreaLightUniformsLib.init()` once at module load, otherwise it's a no-op (no error, just black). Path: `vendor/three/examples/jsm/lights/RectAreaLightUniformsLib.js`.
- `RectAreaLight` does NOT cast shadows in three.js. Pair with a `DirectionalLight` (or `SpotLight`) at the same position/orientation for shadow casting.
- SMAA shaders in r184 are NOT separate files — they're inlined into `SMAAPass.js` (50 KB). The earlier separate `SMAAEdgesShader.js` etc. don't exist anymore. There's a single `SMAAShader.js` if needed externally.
- `GTAOPass.OUTPUT.Default` exists in r184 and is the standard output mode. `gtao.updateGtaoMaterial({ radius, samples, ... })` is how you tune it.
- Three.js postprocessing modules import `three` via bare specifier — works because the page's `importmap` resolves `three` to `./vendor/three/build/three.module.min.js`. The importmap applies to ALL imports in the document, regardless of the importing module's depth.
- `EffectComposer(renderer, renderTarget)` second-arg lets you pass a custom render target with `samples: 4` for MSAA on the intermediate target. This is critical for AA quality before SMAA.
- When using EffectComposer, leave `renderer.toneMapping = ACESFilmicToneMapping` — `OutputPass` reads it and applies tone mapping at the end of the chain. Don't set `NoToneMapping`.
- `ExtrudeGeometry` with `Shape.holes.push(Path)` creates inner walls around holes — required for real "punched-through" geometry like sink drains.
- Default Euler order is `XYZ`, so `rotation.y` is applied before `rotation.z`. For most simple "pivot, then tilt" cases this is fine, but if you need precise nested rotations, use Group hierarchy.
- `RoundedBoxGeometry(w, h, d, segments, radius)` — radius is the LAST arg. Default segments=2 produces near-box; segments=4 gives smoother corners. Auto-radius capped at `min(0.004, minDim * 0.30)` works well for most appliance bodies.

### About Poly Haven asset workflow
- URL pattern: `https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/2k/<slug>/<slug>_<map>_2k.jpg`
- Maps available: `diff` (sRGB albedo), `nor_gl` (linear OpenGL normal), `arm` (linear, R=AO G=Rough B=Metal packed), `disp` (height, optional)
- Slugs that work for kitchen-test: `laminate_floor_02`, `marble_01`, `concrete_floor_03`. Many others 404.
- For three.js wiring: `roughnessMap` and `metalnessMap` can share the same arm texture; three.js samples G and B channels respectively. Material's `roughness` and `metalness` become multipliers (set both to 1.0 to get pure-from-texture values).

## Architecture and design notes

### Single-HTML-file pattern preserved
All work stayed in `kitchen-test.html`. No build step, no bundler, no transpiler. Three.js + addons loaded via `<script type="importmap">` mapping `three` to the vendored `three.module.min.js`. All other addons imported by relative path.

### Material design pattern: PBR + canvas + photo
Three layers of material sourcing, deepest first:
1. **Photo PBR (Poly Haven)** for the dominant visible surfaces: wood, counter stone, floor concrete. Highest quality, real scanned data.
2. **Procedural canvas + derived normal (Sobel)** for surfaces where Poly Haven doesn't have a good fit: brushed stainless, sink dot pattern, backsplash, drywall, microwave grill.
3. **Solid PBR colors** with physical material params for: fridge silver panels (iridescence), copper accents, chrome (faucet, drain), microwave gloss black, cooktop glass (transmission), dish dryer body, etc.

When swapping a procedural surface for photo PBR, the existing `cloneTex(tex, repeatX, repeatY, ...)` helper handles per-instance offsets without touching the underlying GPU texture.

### Postprocessing pipeline
```
RenderPass(scene, camera)         # render scene to MSAA-4 HalfFloat target
  → GTAOPass(16 samples, 0.25 m)  # ground-truth AO for contact shadows
  → UnrealBloomPass(0.14, 0.50, 1.05)  # bloom for emissives only (LCD, fixtures)
  → SMAAPass                      # subpixel-morphological AA
  → OutputPass                    # ACESFilmic + sRGB
```
Renderer's tone mapping setting is NOT disabled — `OutputPass` reads it and applies it at the end.

### Lighting model
Window-dominant + ceiling-fill kitchen daylight:
- **Primary**: window RectAreaLight at 11.2 intensity (was 14 before user's "20% less"), warm color
- **Shadows**: window directional sun at 1.28 (RectAreaLight can't cast)
- **Fill**: 4 ceiling SpotLights at 3.0 each, warm, wide cone, soft penumbra
- **Ambient**: hemi at 0.22, env at 0.55
- **Task**: under-cabinet RectAreaLight at 0.9
- Tone mapping exposure 0.85
- Renderer outputs to MSAA-4 HalfFloat composer target

### Sink architecture
- **Walls**: ExtrudeGeometry of a rounded-rectangle frame Shape (outer rect + inner rect Path hole), extruded 0.18 m downward, beveled top edge 3 mm
- **Bottom plate**: separate ExtrudeGeometry of a rounded-rectangle Shape WITH a circular Path hole (drain). Beveled 6 mm for wall-to-bottom radius
- **Flow channel**: ExtrudeGeometry of a quarter-pie shape, 4.5 cm raised, beveled 4 mm
- **Drain assembly** (in order from top to bottom): chrome strainer ring (RingGeometry on basin floor) → 3 chrome cross-bars → empty 50 mm dark cylindrical pipe (DoubleSide) → sealed circular bottom plate
- **Wire rails**: 2 chrome cylinders along basin top edge near back
- **Wire basket**: 12-mesh chrome wire grid at back-right of basin top, sitting on rails

### Faucet architecture
- **Base puck**: chrome cylinder (30/34 mm tapered, 14 mm tall) on counter
- **Body**: TubeGeometry along 9-point CatmullRomCurve3 — vertical neck (23 cm) → 90° forward arc (9 cm radius) → horizontal reach forward (~25 cm) → downturn (8.5 cm) at end. Tessellation: tubularSegments 192, radialSegments 32. Radius 16.5 mm.
- **Aerator**: cylinder oriented along curve's true end tangent (uses `setFromUnitVectors(+Y, tipTan)`)
- **Outlet disc**: small darker circle at end (oriented via `setFromUnitVectors(+Z, tipTan)` since CircleGeometry's normal is +Z)
- **Handle**: Group with collar (cylinder z-rotated π/2, length 22 mm at lever-local x=0.011) + tapered shaft (length 90 mm) + sphere tip. Lever group origin buried 4 mm INSIDE tube body so collar appears flush. Rotation: y=0.18 (forward), z=18° (upward tilt).
