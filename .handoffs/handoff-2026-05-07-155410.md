## Learnings and discoveries

### Bottom-up climb photos vs. top-down panorama interpretation

The stair photos in `interior-images/stairs/` are a sequential climb capture: photographer started at the bottom (first step), climbed step-by-step, turned to a different angle at each. The room-map.md had described them as a top-down panorama from various positions, which was a misleading framing.

This re-framing is critical for surfacing issues:
- Bottom-up means walls are seen "head-on" from the climber's POV → wall color discrepancies become obvious
- Step-by-step turning means handrail close-ups happen at specific climb positions, not as separate "feature shots" → the handrail's wood grain becomes visible
- Brackets are seen at the climber's eye level repeatedly → the 3-part construction (escutcheon + stem + ferrule) becomes obvious

Future sessions should treat the stair photos as a chronological climb (1 = bottom, 27 = top exit) rather than a flat panorama set.

### Reusing existing helpers and materials saves rework

Phase 7.8 added `bedroomPaintMat = 0xafa299` and `addWallPaint2F` helper for the bedroom taupe paint. Both are reusable for the stair shaft because the hue is the same (warm taupe-grey) and the helper already handles door/window cutouts and per-side paint placement.

## Architecture and design notes

### Stair shaft wall paint pattern

The stair shaft uses the same `bedroomPaintMat` (`0xafa299`) and `addWallPaint2F` helper as the bedrooms. This is intentional — both have the same warm taupe-grey paint per the photos. The helper handles door/window cutouts; for the stair shaft we pass empty arrays (no doors/windows in the shaft walls).

The 6 stair-shaft paint calls split into two y bands:
- 1F band (y=0..F1H) for the lower-flight + winder zone z=[2.90, 4.50]
- 2F band (y=F1H..F1H+F2H) for the upper-flight open shaft z=[3.60, 4.50]

The west exterior wall at x=W is painted in BOTH bands because the upper-flight zone has no 2F floor (open shaft from LDK floor up to 2F ceiling). The 1F band paints the lower portion at z=[2.90, 4.50]; the 2F band paints the upper portion at z=[3.60, 4.50] only (the lower flight's z=[2.90, 3.60] is capped by the 2F floor and doesn't need 2F paint).

### Ceiling material split pattern

Previously a single `matCeiling` with global wood-plank texture. Now split into:
- `matCeiling` — plain plaster (warm off-white, no texture)
- `matPlankCeiling` — same color but with wood-plank texture

`makeCeiling()` defaults to `matCeiling` if no `mat` arg passed. The only call passing the plank material is the stair-shaft footprint at the 2F level.

This pattern can be extended to other room-specific ceilings if photos reveal them. For example, if the LDK turns out to have a different ceiling than the bedrooms, define another material and pass it to specific makeCeiling calls.

### Handrail bracket geometry (3-part construction)

Per stairs-25 photo:
- **Escutcheon disc**: flat chrome plate flush against the wall (radius 22mm, thickness 4mm)
- **Stem cylinder**: short cylinder perpendicular to wall connecting escutcheon to rail (radius 12mm, length 40mm)
- **Ferrule** (NOT YET BUILT): chrome collar wrapping the rail at the bracket point

Currently the build has escutcheon + stem. The ferrule is a future polish item. The escutcheon's centerline matches the stem's centerline (`y = rail_center - HR_R - BR_R`) so they visually connect.

### Bracket y-position derivation

The clean tangent equation for the bracket:
- Rail center at y_rail
- Rail bottom surface at y_rail - HR_R (HR_R = 0.025)
- Bracket top surface at y_bracket_center + BR_R (BR_R = 0.012)
- For tangent contact: y_bracket_center + BR_R = y_rail - HR_R → y_bracket_center = y_rail - HR_R - BR_R = y_rail - 0.037

Previous arbitrary `-0.04` left a 3 mm gap. The new derivation is photo-accurate.
