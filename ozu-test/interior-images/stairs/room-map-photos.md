## what this confirms

- the stair is a true L-shape with 2 winder treads at the bend (per blueprint, `stairs-1`, `stairs-17`).
- the materials are **light maple/birch treads + dark inlay anti-slip nosing strips + white-painted handrail + brushed-chrome wall brackets + taupe paint + white parapet cap + whitewashed wood-plank ceiling above the upper section**. (Earlier "chrome nosing" + "chrome elbow connector" + "light-wood handrail" claims were wrong — corrected after sampling stairs-4, 10, 17, 24.)
- the south end of the 1F lower flight is OPEN to the LDK (no door — the stair carve-out in the LDK's NW corner is a flush opening).
- the handrail bends with the L-shape via a **white-painted elbow connector** (matches the rail), with brushed-chrome wall brackets fixing the rail to the wall — visible in `stairs-7`.

## ceiling and floor

- **ceiling above 2F top**: whitewashed wood-plank, distressed finish (visible at top of `stairs-1`, `stairs-10`, `stairs-13`, `stairs-24`)
- **ceiling above 1F lower flight**: assumed flat painted (the underside of the upper flight forms part of this); not directly sampled — phase D
- **floor at 1F bottom**: light oak plank (continues from LDK; visible `stairs-27` bottom)
- **floor at 2F top**: light oak plank (continues into 2F corridor; not directly sampled — phase D)
