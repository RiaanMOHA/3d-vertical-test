## Base cabinet modules

- Module widths in 15 cm increments: 30, 45, 60, 75, 90 cm
- Standard depth: 65 cm (Taiwan spec, including countertop overhang)
- Counter height: 80–90 cm depending on user height
- Typical ergonomic pairing for a 160 cm user: cooktop at 85 cm, prep area at 90 cm

## Wall cabinets (吊櫃)

Depth 37.5 cm across all variants.

Height options:
- 50 cm (short)
- 60 cm (half-mid)
- 70 cm (mid, most common)
- 90 cm (long)

Width options for non-electric variants: 75, 90, 120, 135 cm. The 120 and 135 cm widths include an internal divider shelf.

## Electric lift wall cabinet with dryer

- Width: 90 cm (also available in 75 cm)
- Depth: 37.5 cm
- Height: 70 cm
- Drying cycle options: 30 / 60 / 90 min
- Plate capacity: about 5 people (75 cm), about 6 people (90 cm)
- Operation: single-button motorised drop to counter height

## Sinks

美·寂靜 super-silent sink (large): W820 × D485 × H183 mm.
美·寂靜 super-silent sink (small, fits 600 mm countertop): W650 × D485 × H183 mm.
JL craftsman deck sink (full-width with integrated drain board): W1615 × D680 × H299 mm, drainage on left, 1 faucet hole.

Material: stainless steel with embossed dot pattern, hydrophilic ceramic coating (e-coat), built-in damping. Front 導流槽 (flow channel) funnels debris to drain.

## Toe-kick drawer (足元抽)

- Interior height: about 21 cm
- Operation: foot-pull, no exposed handle
- Width: matches the cabinet module above it

## Drawer load capacity

- Standard: 20 kg
- Heavy-duty front: 40 kg

## Door panels

Sizes follow cabinet module widths. Five tiers, with finish materials and edge treatments varying by tier.

Tier finishes (from STEDIA door page):

- Stainless + gloss etching + screen print + clear coat (mirror): Stalia Champagne (CVQ), Stalia Wine (CVE). Edge: top and bottom silver ABS caps. Compatible handles: B, F, G.
- Polyolefin decorative film. Edge: four-side frame. Compatible handles: H, J.
- Stainless + screen print + clear coat (mirror, except Beaute Silver): Beaute Silver, Beaute Aqua (CJY), Beaute Rose (CJX), Beaute Bordeaux (CJH). Edge: top and bottom silver ABS caps. Compatible handles: B, F, G.
- Four-side frame resin-coated panel (matte): Blanc Casse (CFQ), Thiollet Rose (CFU), Bleu de Paris (CFV). Edge: resin coat. Compatible handles: H, J.
- Acrylic film vacuum-formed + crystal UV coat (mirror). Edge: five-side seal.

## Cabinet body material

NSSC FW1 stainless (Cleanup proprietary, Cr 14% / Ni 0%). 46% less rare metal than SUS304, 80%+ recyclable. Used inside and out across the STEDIA range. Side and back panels are single-piece formed for ~50% weight reduction versus older constructions.

Industry first: Cleanup launched the first stainless cabinet body kitchen (SAKURA DX) in 1975.

## Hardware

- Drawer runners: Austrian Blum
- Wall cabinet pull-down: rear-storage drop mechanism, front shelf accessible without lowering
- Corner cabinet: pull-out trolley with side triangular trays
