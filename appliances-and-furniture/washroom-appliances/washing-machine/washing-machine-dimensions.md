# washing machine dimensions

Sharp ES-11K1 — drum-type washer-dryer, 11kg wash / 6kg dry, white. The Rakuten listing is the right-opening variant (ES-11K1-WR); a left-opening sibling (ES-11K1-WL) also exists. Released September 18, 2025.

## external dimensions

- width including drain hose: 645 mm
- body width (cabinet only): 595 mm
- depth: 732 mm
- height including water-supply hose: 1100 mm
- body height (cabinet only): 1055 mm

## weight

- ~71 kg

## clearances and installation

- door opens to ~100°
- drain hose exit: left, right, rear, or directly below (3 directions plus underneath)
- minimum drain pan internal depth required: 540 mm
- power cord: ~1.94 m
- earth wire: ~2.54 m
- water-supply hose: ~0.8 m
- drain hose: ~0.8 m

## capacity

- wash and spin: 11 kg
- dry: 6 kg
- water use, wash only: 75 L
- water use, wash to dry: 96 L

## power

- wash: 250 W
- dry: 1,140 W
- energy per cycle, wash: 80 Wh
- energy per cycle, wash to dry: 1,880 Wh

## cycle times

- wash 11 kg: ~45 min
- wash to dry 6 kg: ~150 min

## noise

- wash: 26 dB
- spin: 39 dB
- dry: 41 dB
