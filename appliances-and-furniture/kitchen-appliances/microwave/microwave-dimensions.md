# microwave dimensions

Sharp RE-TS174 (black variant: RE-TS174-B) — single-function microwave, 17L flat-type interior, side-opening door, hertz-free (50/60 Hz). Released November 16, 2023.

## external dimensions

- width: 460 mm
- depth: 350 mm (390 mm including door handle)
- depth with door fully open: 690 mm
- height: 280 mm

## interior usable dimensions

- width: 275 mm
- depth: 325 mm
- height: 180 mm
- type: flat (no turntable)

## weight

- ~12 kg

## clearance requirements

- sides: 5 cm minimum each
- top: 15 cm minimum
- rear: 10 cm minimum (measured from rear protrusion)

## capacity and output

- total cavity volume: 17 L
- microwave output: 650 W (60 Hz) / 520 W (50 Hz), plus 500 W and 200 W equivalent settings
- continuous rated high-frequency output: 350 W
- rated power consumption: 950 W (50 Hz) / 1300 W (60 Hz)
- power supply: AC 100 V, 50/60 Hz

## features

- absolute humidity ("rakuchin") sensor for auto-warming
- 5 auto menus
- twin dial controls
- backlit LCD display
- door opens to the side
