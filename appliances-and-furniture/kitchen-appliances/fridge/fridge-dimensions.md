# fridge dimensions

Panasonic NR-E45RY3L-S — 450L, 5-door, 60cm width, left-opening, silver. Top-unit compressor design ("compact big" series). Released January 2026.

## external dimensions

- width: 600 mm
- depth: 648 mm
- height: 1850 mm
- required installation depth (door clearance): 648 mm

## weight

- 91 kg

## capacity

- total rated capacity: 450 L
- refrigerator compartment: 230 L (169 L usable)
- partial / chilled switching compartment: 12 L
- ice maker compartment: 19 L (5 L usable)
- freezer (upper): 18 L (8 L usable)
- vegetable compartment: 93 L (60 L usable)
- freezer (lower): 90 L (59 L usable)

## power

- annual energy use: 266 kWh / year (50 Hz and 60 Hz)
- compressor: 92 W
- defrost heater: 166 W

## door configuration

- 5 doors total
- main refrigerator: left-opening (left-hinged)
- vegetable compartment: full-open pull-out drawer with bearing rails
- freezer compartment: full-open pull-out drawer with bearing rails
